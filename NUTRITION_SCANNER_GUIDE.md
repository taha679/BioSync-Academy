# 🍽️ Nutrition Scanner — Complete User Guide

## Overview
The **Nutrition Scanner** is a powerful new feature integrated into your Health-Sync AI wellness app. It allows you to scan any food product's barcode using your device camera or manually enter the barcode to instantly retrieve comprehensive nutritional information.

---

## Features

### ✅ **Dual Input Modes**
1. **📷 Camera Mode** — Real-time barcode scanning
2. **📝 Manual Mode** — Type barcode directly

### ✅ **Comprehensive Food Data**
- **Product Information**: Name, brand, barcode, product image
- **Nutrition Facts**: Energy, fats, proteins, carbohydrates (per 100g)
- **Detailed Nutrients**: Sugars, salt, fiber, calcium, iron, sodium, potassium
- **Allergen Warnings**: Clear allergen detection and labeling
- **Nutri-Score**: Food quality rating (A=Excellent to E=Poor)

### ✅ **Health Integration**
- Log scanned products directly to your health profile
- Track food consumption data
- Monitor nutritional intake over time
- Earn XP for logging food products

---

## How to Use

### **Step 1: Access the Scanner**
- Tap the **"Nutrition"** button in the bottom navigation bar
- You'll see the scanner page with two mode options

### **Step 2: Choose Your Input Method**

#### **Camera Mode** (Recommended)
1. Click **"📷 Camera"** button
2. Click **"📷 Start Camera"** 
3. Point your device at a product barcode
4. Wait for the barcode to be detected
5. Product information loads automatically

#### **Manual Mode** (Fallback)
1. Click **"📝 Manual"** button
2. Enter the product barcode in the input field
3. Click **"🔍 Search Product"**
4. Wait for product data to load

### **Step 3: View Product Information**
Once scanned, you'll see:
- **Product Details** — Image, name, brand, barcode
- **Nutrition Facts Grid** — Key nutrients at a glance
- **Detailed Nutrients** — Full nutritional breakdown
- **Allergen Alerts** — ⚠️ Warnings if applicable
- **Nutri-Score** — Color-coded health rating

### **Step 4: Take Action**

#### **📝 Log to Health**
- Click the **"📝 Log to Health"** button
- Product data syncs to your health profile
- Earn XP for logging
- Data appears in your nutrition tracking

#### **🔄 Scan Another**
- Click **"🔄 Scan Another"** to scan a different product
- Camera/manual mode remains active
- Continue scanning multiple items

## Understanding Nutrition Information

### **Nutrition Facts (Per 100g)**

| Nutrient | Unit | What It Means |
|----------|------|--------------|
| **Energy** | kcal | Calories per 100g |
| **Fat** | g | Total fat content |
| **Protein** | g | Protein content for muscle building |
| **Carbs** | g | Carbohydrate content for energy |
| **Sugars** | g | Simple sugar content |
| **Salt** | g | Sodium content (health indicator) |
| **Fiber** | g | Dietary fiber for digestion |

### **Nutri-Score Explained**
- 🟢 **A** — Excellent nutritional quality
- 🟡 **B** — Good nutritional quality
- 🟠 **C** — Fair nutritional quality
- 🔴 **D** — Poor nutritional quality
- 🔴 **E** — Very poor nutritional quality

### **Allergens**
Common allergens tracked:
- Gluten, Dairy, Nuts, Soy
- Eggs, Fish, Shellfish, Sesame
- Mustard, Celery, Lupin, Mollusks

## Data Sources

The app uses the **Open Food Facts API** — a free, crowdsourced database of food products with:
- 1M+ products worldwide
- Detailed nutritional information
- Community-verified data
- No API key required
- Fully offline-capable (data cached locally)

**API**: https://www.openfoodfacts.org/

## Tips & Tricks

### 🎯 **Best Practices**
1. **Good Lighting** — Ensure the barcode is well-lit for faster scanning
2. **Steady Hand** — Hold device steady while scanning
3. **Different Angles** — Try rotating the product if first scan fails
4. **Clean Barcodes** — Avoid scanning damaged or obscured barcodes

### 📊 **Use Cases**
- **Shopping** — Compare nutrition of similar products before buying
- **Dieting** — Track calories and macronutrients
- **Allergies** — Quickly check for allergens before eating
- **Health Goals** — Monitor protein, fiber, and sugar intake
- **Wellness Tracking** — Log foods with other health metrics

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
- Scan products **before** eating to understand nutrition
- Use **Manual Mode** if camera fails
- Log frequently for better health insights
- Check **Nutri-Score** for quick quality assessment

## Troubleshooting

### ❌ **Camera Not Working**
- ✅ Check browser camera permissions
- ✅ Grant camera access to the app
- ✅ Use Manual Mode as fallback
- ✅ Try on a different browser

### ❌ **Barcode Not Found**
- ✅ Try a different product
- ✅ Check barcode is readable
- ✅ Use Manual Mode to enter code
- ✅ Product may not be in the database

### ❌ **Incomplete Nutrition Data**
- ✅ Some older products have less data
- ✅ International products may vary
- ✅ Available data will still display
- ✅ Check "Detailed Nutrients" for more info
